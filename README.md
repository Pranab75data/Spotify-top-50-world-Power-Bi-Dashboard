
# 🎧 Spotify Power BI Dashboard

## 📌 Business Requirement  
Spotify stakeholders — including music analysts, playlist managers, and marketing teams — require a **consolidated Power BI dashboard** to monitor song and artist performance across multiple dimensions.  

### **Dashboard Pages & Features**

#### **1. Overview Page**
- Track key KPIs: **Total Songs, Distinct Artists, Avg Popularity, Avg Duration**  
- Compare **Explicit vs Non-Explicit** songs  
- Analyze songs by **Album Type** (Single, Album, Compilation)  
- View **Distinct Songs** and **Avg Popularity by Year**  
- Identify trends of **Avg Popularity & Distinct Songs by Month**  
- Highlight **Top Songs** and **Top Artists** by Popularity  

#### **2. Artist Page**
- Display **Top Artists** by Popularity  
- Compare **Tracks per Album** and **Songs by Artist**  
- Enable **drill-downs** to artist-level data (songs, release date, avg popularity, avg position, duration)  
- Identify artists with **consistent hits** and **#1 positions**  

#### **3. Songs Page**
- Rank **Top Songs by Popularity**  
- Show **Tracks per Song** (Album/Single distribution)  
- Compare **Songs by Song Count**  
- Provide a detailed **song-level table** (song name, release date, distinct artists, avg popularity, position, duration per year)  

---

## 📊 Problem Statement  
The raw Spotify “Top 50” dataset only contains basic rankings, making it challenging for stakeholders to identify deeper trends and actionable insights.  

### **Key Problems Solved**
- **KPI Monitoring:** Quick visibility into songs, artists, popularity, and duration.  
- **Explicit vs Non-Explicit Analysis:** Clear comparison of content types and performance.  
- **Distribution Insight:** Breakdown by album type and release year for better understanding.  
- **Trend Tracking:** Popularity and song trends shown over time (monthly & yearly).  
- **Connected Insights:** Drill-downs connect overview insights with artist and song-level details.  
- **Better Decision-Making:** Helps marketing and curation teams identify high-performing artists, trending songs, and audience preferences.  

---

## 🧩 Tech Stack
- **Tool:** Microsoft Power BI  
- **Data Source:** Spotify Top 50 Dataset (CSV)  
- **Data Cleaning:** Power Query  
- **Visualizations:** KPI Cards, Bar Charts, Line Charts, Tables, Drill-throughs  

---

## 🚀 Outcome
An **interactive, data-driven dashboard** that empowers Spotify teams to track music performance, analyze trends, and make informed marketing and playlist decisions efficiently.  

---

## 🛠️ How to Use
1. Download the dataset (`spotify_top50.csv`).  
2. Open the `.pbix` Power BI file.  
3. Load the dataset using **Power Query Editor**.  
4. Refresh visuals to explore interactive insights.
